# Next Mission: Setup: Muse and Claude (Tier A/B)

You can run Next Mission with Muse (this app) or in a Claude Project. No paid Grok Bot required. What you get depends on how you set it up: full behavior where files and scheduling are available, and the complete interview and checklist everywhere.

## Option 1: In this app (Muse)

1. Say: **"Start my Next Mission."**
2. Muse pulls the nine working files from the public repository (github.com/gabe-mobius/next-mission) and runs the source checks on them.
3. Answer the interview, one question at a time: branch, retiring or separating, your date, spouse and kids, plans, where you'll live, reminder preferences.
4. You get your personal checklist as a document to keep, every item with its official source, quote, and date checked. If the GI Bill transfer deadline applies to you, you will hear about it in this first conversation.
5. For reminders, ask for scheduled check-ins when you set up; otherwise check in any time and ask **"What's due?"**

## Option 2: A Claude Project (claude.ai)

1. Create a new Project and name it Next Mission.
2. Paste the full text of `ASSISTANT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` (from the repository) into the project's custom instructions.
3. Download the files from the repository and add these to the project's knowledge: `MASTER_CHECKLIST.md`, `STATE_BENEFITS.md`, `profile.example.json`.
4. Start a chat in the project and say: **"Start my Next Mission."**
5. Answer the interview. Keep the checklist document Muse gives you; bring it back to the project whenever you return.

## Staying current

- The master checklist is re-verified and republished monthly. On the Project path, download the newer `MASTER_CHECKLIST.md` and `STATE_BENEFITS.md` and replace the project files when you want a refresh, then ask Muse **"What changed for me?"**
- Claude cannot promise proactive reminders on the Project path. If that matters to you, the Grok Bot build (Tier A) or check-ins here in the app are the reliable routes.

## Good to know

- Your profile holds personal details. Keep it in your own files; never post it publicly.
- This is general information, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Confirm move rules with your installation transportation office.
